Photos from your phone look great on the TV.

Very small photos, such as ones saved from a text message or a website, can look blurry. Curator tells you if a photo is too small.

Add photos to your Frame Pick from your camera roll or files.

How should they fit?

Fill screen trims the edges. Tap Adjust on a photo to choose what stays.

On your Frame

Tap a photo to choose it. The TV shows one photo at a time. You can remove only the photos you added with Curator.

Activity

Open Curator on another phone

Point the other phone's camera at this code, then tap the link that appears. The phone must be on your home Wi-Fi.

Put Curator on your home screen

iPhone (Safari)

  1. Tap the Share button (a square with an arrow).
  2. Tap “Add to Home Screen”.
  3. Tap “Add”.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Tap the menu button (three dots).
  2. Tap “Add to Home screen”.
  3. Tap “Add”.

Your name

Curator shows your name next to the photo you choose for the TV. It is only a label. There is no login.

Appearance

System follows your phone's light or dark setting.

This is Samsung art. Curator can show it but cannot remove it. Use the TV to remove it.
